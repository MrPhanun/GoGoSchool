Learning Programs

A program for every age & stage

Sunflower Room

Ages 2–3 · Gentle routines, sensory play, and first steps toward independence.

  • 🧸 Sensory & motor skill play
  • 🗣️ Early language building
  • 😴 Structured rest & snack times
🌈

Rainbow Room

Ages 4–5 · Story-driven learning, early numeracy, and lots of creative expression.

  • 🔤 Phonics & pre-reading
  • 🔢 Early math concepts
  • 🎨 Art, music & movement
⭐

Star Room

Ages 5–6 · Kindergarten readiness with a focus on reading, writing, and social skills.

  • 📖 Reading & writing foundations
  • 🧮 Problem solving & logic
  • 🤝 Teamwork & leadership
